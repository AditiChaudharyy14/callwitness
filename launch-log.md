## How to use this

Keep this file open in a second window. After each reply you make, add a line.
Five fields, most of them one word. If you don't know a field, write `?` — a `?`
is data too, and on Tuesday it tells you what to ask.

**prod** — do they run agents on real work, or is this a side project?
`yes` / `no` / `?`

**sector** — if they run agents on real work, what for? Free text, one or two
words. Watch for: hiring, screening, CV, credit, lending, insurance, underwriting.
Those are the vertical and they matter more than everything else here.

**MCP** — do they actually use MCP? `yes` / `no` / `?`
**Count the `no`s.** That number decides how fast October's non-MCP recorder gets
built, and it is the single most valuable thing tonight produces.

**who answers** — when their agent does something wrong, who comes to them asking
what happened? Most people won't say. `?` is fine and common.

**ran it** — did they say they installed it or would? `yes` / `said they would` / `no`
